# How to Log In — Customer Portal (Zan-F Power Systems / RECD Tracker)

Customer sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your email — there's no password to remember.

## Steps

1. Go to the login page: **https://admin-web-three-blush.vercel.app/login**
2. Click the **Customer** tab (next to "Staff Login" and "Vendor").
3. Enter the email address your Zan-F sales contact has on file for you, and click **Get One-Time Password**.
4. Check your inbox for an email from Zan-F Power Systems with the subject **"Your Zan-F Power Systems sign-in code"**. It contains a 6-digit code.
   - The email arrives within a minute or two. If you don't see it, check your Spam/Junk folder.
   - The code expires **10 minutes** after it's sent — if it expires, just click "Get One-Time Password" again for a fresh one.
5. Enter the 6-digit code on the login page and click **Verify & Sign In**.

That's it — you're in. From here you can view your orders, site status, and raise complaints.

## If it doesn't work

- **"If that email is registered, an OTP has been sent to it" but nothing arrives**: this message shows either way, whether or not the email matches your account — it's intentionally generic. If you never receive a code, the most likely cause is that the email you typed doesn't exactly match what's on file with Zan-F (a different email address entirely, a typo, or an account that hasn't been set up yet). Contact your Zan-F sales contact to confirm the exact email on your account.
- **Code says "Invalid or expired"**: request a new one — codes expire after 10 minutes and can only be used once.
- Still stuck? Contact your Zan-F sales/account contact — they can check your account from their side (see the companion admin guide, "Enabling Customer Login").
